# Geometric Friendly

Warm, approachable consumer-product type system (`Plus Jakarta Sans` + `Nunito` + `DM Mono`) with gentle rounded terminals and comfortable reading cadence.

## Font Pairing Rationale
- **Display (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Crisp geometric clarity with friendly open counters.
- **Body (`Nunito`):** Soft rounded terminals add warmth to onboarding flows and consumer cards.
- **Mono (`DM Mono`):** Gentle low-contrast monospace for chips and numeric badges.

## OpenType & Numeric Rules
- Keep body line-height generous (`1.64`) and display tracking slightly tight (`-0.02em`).
- Enforce `tabular-nums` on prices, ratings, and availability counters.
