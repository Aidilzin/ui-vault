# Architectural Slab

Sturdy journalistic slab-serif display voice (`Zilla Slab`) grounded by clean `Inter` body prose and `Space Mono` index stamps.

## Font Pairing Rationale
- **Display (`Zilla Slab`):** Contemporary mechanical slab serifs provide authoritative editorial structure without feeling Victorian.
- **Body (`Inter`):** Neutral, legible screen body copy.
- **Mono (`Space Mono`):** Geometric monospace stamps for publication indices and issue dates.

## OpenType & Numeric Rules
- Keep `Zilla Slab` at weights `600–700` for display headings (`-0.015em` tracking).
- Uppercase index labels enforce `letter-spacing >= 0.08em`.
