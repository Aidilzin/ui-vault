# Wide Kinetic Display

High-impact contemporary neo-grotesque pairing (`Clash Display` + `Satoshi` via Fontshare) engineered for bold product launches, spatial UIs, and kinetic studio heroes.

## Font Pairing Rationale
- **Display (`Clash Display`):** Wide geometric proportions with razor-clean squared apertures command attention at hero scale.
- **Body (`Satoshi`):** Crisp modernist sans with balanced counter-shapes for tight UI copy and subheads.

## OpenType & Numeric Rules
- Tighten display tracking (`-0.025em`) and keep hero line-height compact (`1.02`).
- Apply `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to KPI counters and pricing tiers.
