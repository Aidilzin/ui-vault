# Editorial Didone

High-contrast optical Didone display voice (`Bodoni Moda`) paired with clean geometric `Plus Jakarta Sans` body copy and `IBM Plex Mono` metadata.

## Font Pairing Rationale
- **Display (`Bodoni Moda`):** Optical-size Didone serifs deliver razor-sharp thick/thin stroke contrast suited to luxury showcases and exhibition mastheads.
- **Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Neutral geometric warmth keeps long-form captions effortless to read.
- **Mono (`IBM Plex Mono`):** Calibrated gallery plaque numbers and edition codes.

## OpenType & Numeric Rules
- Keep `Bodoni Moda` at display scale (`>= 24px`) so hairlines never alias at small sizes.
- Apply `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to dates, prices, and plate numbers.
- Uppercase eyebrows enforce `letter-spacing >= 0.08em`.
