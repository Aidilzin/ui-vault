# 8-Bit Arcade / Pixel HUD

Chunky retro game-console aesthetic inspired by `@8bitcn` (clean-room specification), defined by stepped pixel-notched borders, high-contrast CRT navy canvases, and vibrant 8-bit status bars.

## Core Tokens & Surfaces
- **Canvas & Surfaces:** Deep arcade navy (`#111226`), stepped dialog boxes (`#1c1e3a`), and crisp `2px` pixel-stepped white/cyan borders (`#ffffff`).
- **Ink:** Pure pixel white (`#ffffff`) and muted HUD lavender (`#a4a8d4`).
- **Accents:** 8-bit gold (`#ffd43b`), health-bar emerald (`#40c057`), and coin-slot crimson (`#fa5252`).

## Typography & Layout
- Pixel-crisp monospace uppercase labels, `0px` corners with stepped `box-shadow` pixel bevels, and segmented health/progress blocks.

## Forbidden Anti-Patterns
- No antialiased soft blur, smooth glassmorphism, or rounded `12px+` modern SaaS cards.
