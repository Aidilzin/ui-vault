# Blueprint Cyanotype

Prussian engineering cyanotype cobalt canvas with crisp drafting-white 1px hairlines, calibrated callout markers, and architectural precision.

## Core Tokens & Surfaces
- **Cyanotype Ground:** Deep Prussian blueprint cobalt (`#0b2545`) with tonal exposure surfaces (`#10315a` / `#163e70`).
- **Drafting Ink:** Crisp chalk-white primary ink (`#e8f1ff`), technical blueprint cyan (`#7dd3fc`), and amber drafting-pencil callout accent (`#fbbf24`).
- **Geometry:** Zero-blur `0px–2px` corners, exposed `1px` construction lines (`rgba(232, 241, 255, 0.24)`), and tabular monospace annotations.

## Motion Rules
- Use crisp technical snapping (`120ms–180ms`) and stroke-draw progressions evocative of pen plotters and drafting tools.
- Never apply squishy elastic bounce to technical drafting callouts.

## Forbidden Anti-Patterns
- Never introduce warm parchment beige, rounded pill fluff, or heavy photorealistic gradients.
- Maintain high contrast (`>= 8:1`) between drafting-white ink and Prussian cobalt ground.
