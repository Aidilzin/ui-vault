# Zine Wire / Cyber Editorial Feed

High-voltage digital magazine and breaking-feed aesthetic built on pitch charcoal grounds, electric mint highlighter underlines, hyper-violet structural accents, and dense asymmetric story grids.

## Core Tokens & Surfaces
- **Canvas & Surfaces:** Deep charcoal (`#131313`), stepped feed cards (`#2d2d2d`), and sharp `1px` rules (`#313131`).
- **Ink:** Stark white headlines (`#ffffff`), cool gray decks (`#949494`), and high-contrast inverted tags (`#000000` on `#3cffd0`).
- **Accents:** Electric cyber-mint (`#3cffd0`) for live stamps and highlights, paired with hyper-violet (`#5200ff`) for feature bands and category ribbons.

## Typography & Layout
- Oversized tight-tracked display headlines paired with bold uppercase monospace timestamps and bylines.
- Sharp `0px–2px` radii and hard color-blocked category badges.

## Forbidden Anti-Patterns
- No soft translucent glass cards or gentle pastel backgrounds.
- Never apply neon mint (`#3cffd0`) to long body paragraphs — reserve it for metadata pills, underlines, and live badges.
