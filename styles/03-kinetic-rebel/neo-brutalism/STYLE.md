# Neo-Brutalism / Anti-Design

Unapologetically raw, tactile web design that exaggerates structural primitives with thick ink outlines, hard offset shadows, and bold flat color blocks.

## Core Tokens & Surfaces
- **Borders:** Thick `2px–3px` solid `#000000` strokes on every card, button, input, and badge.
- **Shadows:** Hard zero-blur offset drop shadows (`4px 4px 0 #000000` resting, `2px 2px 0 #000000` with `translate(2px, 2px)` on active press).
- **Palette:** Warm off-white canvas (`#fffdf5`) paired with saturated flat fills—acid yellow (`#ffde00`), coral (`#ff5c5c`), electric blue (`#3b82f6`), mint (`#22c55e`).

## Typography & Layout
- Heavy geometric display headings (`Space Grotesk`, `Syne`, or `Archivo Black`) paired with utilitarian monospace tags.
- Deliberate tension: slight badge tilts (`-2deg`), dense sticker clusters, and chunky exposed borders.

## Forbidden Anti-Patterns
- No soft blurred shadows (`box-shadow` blur radius must stay `0px`), translucent glass, or subtle low-contrast gray-on-gray text.
