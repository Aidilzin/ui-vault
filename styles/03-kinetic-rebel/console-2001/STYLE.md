# Console 2001 / Kinetic Cartridge UI

High-energy early-2000s interactive entertainment aesthetic combining molded metallic gray shells, saturated primary red/amber/cobalt action modules, and chunky pill navigation tabs.

## Core Tokens & Surfaces
- **Canvas & Surfaces:** Molded light-alloy gray (`#dedede`) or dark-slate stage (`#21242e`), crisp white content bays (`#ffffff`), and cobalt structural trims (`#3d4f97`).
- **Accents:** Primary arcade red (`#e60012`), solar amber (`#f68d1f`), and ice-blue highlights (`#9fbee7`).
- **Geometry:** Chunky `8px–12px` molded corners paired with full-pill navigation buttons and crisp `2px` color-coded borders.

## Typography & Layout
- Bold geometric display headlines paired with compact uppercase technical spec tags and high-contrast modular tiles.

## Forbidden Anti-Patterns
- No whisper-quiet low-contrast gray-on-gray minimalism or fragile hairline typography.
- Avoid desaturated muddy tones; keep primary accents crisp and high-chroma.
