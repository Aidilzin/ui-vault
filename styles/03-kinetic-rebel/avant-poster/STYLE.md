# Avant Poster / Experimental Archive

Rule-breaking experimental web archive combining raw light-gray newsprint grounds, stark black ink, and a single high-voltage electric violet signal.

## Core Tokens & Surfaces
- **Canvas & Surfaces:** Cool archival newsprint (`#f1f1f1`), crisp white inset blocks (`#ffffff`), and hard `#000000` structural dividers.
- **Ink:** Pure black (`#000000`) and deep charcoal (`#101010`).
- **Accent:** Electric ultraviolet (`#711bff`) for active hovers, index markers, and experimental callouts.

## Typography & Layout
- Expressive oversized display headings with abrupt scale contrast against compact index numbers and sharp `0px–5px` containers.

## Motion Rules
- Direct `300ms ease` state swaps paired with bold hover reveals and zero subtle SaaS blur.

## Forbidden Anti-Patterns
- No translucent glass panels, soft drop shadows, or timid pastel gradients.
