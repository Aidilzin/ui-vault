# Acid Graphics & Y2K Chrome

Maximalist cyber-rave and late-90s digital print aesthetic combining pitch-black backdrops, liquid metallic gradients, stretched display type, and high-voltage accents.

## Core Tokens & Surfaces
- **Canvas:** Pure void black (`#050507`) with subtle wireframe grids or starburst vector registration marks.
- **Accents:** Electric slime (`#ccff00`), hyper-magenta (`#ff00a8`), and silver-chrome specular bands (`linear-gradient(135deg, #ffffff 0%, #7c8496 45%, #f8fafc 55%, #272b36 100%)`).
- **Details:** Micro-barcode strips, stretched pill badges, wireframed globes, and technical serial numbers.

## Typography & Layout
- Wide/extended grotesque or spiky display typography for heroes, balanced against tiny uppercase monospace (`10px–11px`) technical specs so content remains scannable.

## Forbidden Anti-Patterns
- Never apply chrome or neon colors to long body paragraphs—reserve maximalist effects strictly for headlines, frames, and hero badges.
