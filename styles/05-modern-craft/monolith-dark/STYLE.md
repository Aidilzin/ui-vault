# Monolith Dark / Precision Issue Canvas

Ultra-restrained nocturnal product interface built on near-black stepped charcoal surfaces, whisper-quiet `1px` borders, crisp neo-grotesk hierarchy, and disciplined indigo focus signals.

## Core Tokens & Surfaces
- **Canvas & Surfaces:** Deep charcoal canvas (`#0f1011`), stepped panels (`#141516`, `#191a1b`), and subtle hairline borders (`#23252a`).
- **Ink:** High-contrast crisp off-white (`#f7f8f8`), cool slate secondary (`#d0d6e0`), and quiet metadata (`#8a8f98`, `#62666d`).
- **Accent:** Restrained indigo (`#5e6ad2`, hover `#828fff`) reserved for active selections, keyboard focus rings, and primary actions.

## Typography & Layout
- Single geometric sans (`Inter`) with tight heading tracking (`-0.025em`) and `tabular-nums` on every issue ID, shortcut hint, and counter.
- Crisp `6px–8px` card radii, `4px` button/input radii, and keyboard-first list rows separated by `1px` rules.

## Forbidden Anti-Patterns
- No loud multi-color rainbow badges, heavy outer drop shadows, or bright white hero blocks.
- Keep surface elevation steps subtle (`2%–4%` luminance delta) rather than stark gray jumps.
