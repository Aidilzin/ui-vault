# Kinetic Studio White

Pure alabaster micro-interaction showcase canvas with whisper-soft 1px pill elevation, tactile spring feedback, and disciplined Apple/macOS-grade restraint.

## Core Tokens & Surfaces
- **Canvas:** Alabaster studio ground (`#fafafa`) paired with pure white elevated cards (`#ffffff`) and subtle cool-gray inset wells (`#f2f3f5`).
- **Borders & Elevation:** Hairline `1px` borders (`#e4e4e7`) paired with ultra-soft ambient lift (`0 4px 16px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`).
- **Accent:** Crisp electric cobalt (`#0066ff`) for active pills and spring toggles; deep carbon (`#111113`) for primary ink.

## Motion Rules
- Use short, critically damped springs (`180ms–260ms`) for pill switches, button presses, and drawer expansions.
- Every interactive affordance should respond on pointer-down with subtle scale compression (`0.98`).

## Forbidden Anti-Patterns
- Never add heavy dark drop shadows, harsh 2px black outlines, or neon glow halos on light alabaster.
- Avoid dense multi-column walls of text without generous breathing gutters.
