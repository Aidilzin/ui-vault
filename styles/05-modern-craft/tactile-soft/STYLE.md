# Tactile Soft / Warm Elevation Craft

Approachable, high-trust consumer product aesthetic built on pure white cards, ultra-soft diffuse elevation, friendly pill filters, and a vibrant coral-rose action accent.

## Core Tokens & Surfaces
- **Canvas & Surfaces:** Pure white canvas (`#ffffff`), soft neutral hairline borders (`#dddddd`), and gentle diffuse shadows (`0 6px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`).
- **Ink:** Deep warm charcoal (`#222222`), readable mid-gray (`#6a6a6a`), and subtle caption gray (`#929292`).
- **Accent:** Vibrant coral-rose (`#ff385c`, hover `#e00b41`) reserved for primary CTAs, active search triggers, and saved-state highlights.

## Typography & Layout
- Friendly geometric sans with generous `12px–16px` card radii, full-pill (`9999px`) filter chips, and clear 4:3 media framing.

## Forbidden Anti-Patterns
- No harsh `0px` sharp brutalist corners, pitch-black cyberpunk glows, or dense monospace HUD telemetry.
