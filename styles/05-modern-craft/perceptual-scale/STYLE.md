# Perceptual Scale / 12-Step System

Mathematical 12-step perceptual color architecture distilled from Radix Themes (MIT License), engineered for predictable solid, soft, surface, and outline component contrast across every interactive state.

## Core Tokens & Surfaces
- **Step 1–3 Surfaces:** Crisp app background (`#fcfcfd`), subtle panel tint (`#f9f9fb`), and interactive hover surface (`#eff0f3`).
- **Step 6–7 Borders:** Subtle hairline (`#d8d9e0`) and focus-ready interactive border (`#cdced7`).
- **Step 9–12 Accent & Ink:** Solid iris accent (`#5b5bd6`, high-contrast text `#1c2024`, secondary `#60646c`).

## Typography & Layout
- Clean sans scale with predictable `6px–8px` radius tokens and accessible `2px` focus rings.

## Forbidden Anti-Patterns
- Never skip contrast pairings between background steps (1–3) and foreground ink steps (11–12).
