# Aurora Mesh / Nocturnal Glow Craft

Dark-first developer marketing aesthetic combining pitch obsidian canvases, razor-sharp `1px` silver hairlines, and restrained luminous aurora edge blooms in solar amber, azure, and emerald.

## Core Tokens & Surfaces
- **Canvas & Surfaces:** Deep obsidian ground (`#06060a`), elevated matte panels (`#0a0a0c`, `#101012`), and subtle metallic borders (`#26282b`).
- **Ink:** Crisp ice-white (`#fcfdff`), cool pewter body (`#a1a4a5`), and muted technical captions (`#888e90`).
- **Accents:** Restrained luminous aurora spectrum (`#3b9eff` azure, `#11ff99` emerald, `#ff801f` solar amber) applied only to ambient hero backdrops and thin specular edge rims.

## Typography & Layout
- Sleek tight-tracked display headlines paired with clean monospace API/SDK snippets and crisp `8px` card radii.

## Forbidden Anti-Patterns
- Never flood body copy or dense tables with neon gradients; confine aurora luminance strictly to hero horizons and thin `1px` perimeter highlights.
