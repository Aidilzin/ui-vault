# Monochrome Stark / Binary Grid

Hyper-minimalist developer platform aesthetic built on pure white and obsidian binary contrast, hairline `1px` grid cells, and a single surgical blue focus signal.

## Core Tokens & Surfaces
- **Canvas & Surfaces:** Pure white canvas (`#ffffff`), subtle off-white elevation (`#fafafa`, `#f5f5f5`), and crisp `1px` neutral borders (`#ebebeb`).
- **Ink:** Deep obsidian (`#171717`), mid-slate body (`#4d4d4d`), and quiet technical captions (`#888888`).
- **Accents:** Binary black CTA (`#171717` on white) with electric blue (`#0070f3`) reserved for focus rings and inline links, plus stark red (`#ee0000`) for errors.

## Typography & Layout
- Tight-tracked neo-grotesk (`Inter`) with `-0.04em` hero headlines paired with clean monospace CLI snippets and deployment hashes.
- Exposed hairline grid intersections and crisp `6px` radii with zero drop shadows.

## Forbidden Anti-Patterns
- No decorative background gradients, colorful illustrations, or heavy elevation shadows.
- Never dilute the binary black/white hierarchy with multiple competing brand hues.
