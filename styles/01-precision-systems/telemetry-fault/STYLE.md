# Telemetry Fault / Deep Plum Console

High-density observability and error-triage interface pairing deep aubergine-plum dark surfaces with unmistakable electric lime and rose diagnostic signals.

## Core Tokens & Surfaces
- **Canvas & Surfaces:** Deep plum-black canvas (`#150f23`), elevated diagnostic panels (`#1f1633`), and structural plum borders (`#362d59`).
- **Ink:** Pure white primary (`#ffffff`), cool lavender-gray secondary (`#cfcfdb`), and muted telemetry labels (`#79628c`).
- **Accents:** Violet primary (`#6a5fc1`), electric lime nominal/resolved (`#c2ef4e`), and vivid rose fault/regression (`#fa7faa`).

## Typography & Layout
- Clean sans headings paired with monospace stack traces, HTTP status tags, and dense `tabular-nums` sparkline counters.
- Crisp `6px` panels with tight `1px` borders and zero blurry glow clouds.

## Forbidden Anti-Patterns
- Never use electric lime (`#c2ef4e`) or rose (`#fa7faa`) as full-page backgrounds — keep them strictly for status chips, trace markers, and delta badges.
- Avoid soft pastel gradients that reduce stack-trace legibility.
