# Neo-Swiss Grid & Kinetic Editorial

Mathematical poster clarity built on exposed hairline grids, extreme typographic scale contrast, and disciplined negative space.

## Core Tokens & Surfaces
- **Palette:** High-contrast bone/paper (`#f5f4f0`) with pitch ink (`#111111`), or inverted obsidian (`#0d0d0d`) with chalk ink (`#f2f2f2`), plus one stark International Orange (`#ff3b00`) or Cobalt (`#0047ff`) signal accent.
- **Structure:** Visible `1px` full-bleed column and row rules (`rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.14)` on light, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` on dark). Zero border-radius (`0px`) on layout containers.
- **Hierarchy:** Scale and alignment do all the work—no cards, no elevation shadows, no gradient fills.

## Typography & Layout
- Single Neo-Grotesk typeface family (`Inter`, `Neue Haas Grotesk`, or `Helvetica Neue`) across massive tight-tracked display headlines (`-0.04em`, line-height `0.95`) and crisp 12–14px metadata captions.
- Asymmetric 12-column grids where empty grid cells are intentionally left blank to frame copy.

## Forbidden Anti-Patterns
- No rounded cards, soft drop shadows, glass blur, or decorative background blobs.
- No more than one accent hue per screen.
