# Transit Precision / Rail Signage System

High-clarity European rail and wayfinding aesthetic distilled from the DB UX Design System (Apache-2.0 License), built for instantaneous scannability with AAA contrast, tabular timetables, and a single signal-red wayfinding bar.

## Core Tokens & Surfaces
- **Canvas & Surfaces:** Clean signage white (`#ffffff`), cool station gray (`#f0f3f5`), and crisp `1px` schedule rules (`#c7cfd6`).
- **Ink:** Deep charcoal signage ink (`#282d37`) and neutral timetable metadata (`#646973`).
- **Accents:** Signal rail crimson (`#ec0016`) for top-bar wayfinding and delay alerts, paired with clear operational green (`#408335`) for on-time status.

## Typography & Layout
- Bold condensed/neo-grotesk station headers paired with strict `tabular-nums` departure columns and sharp `4px` modules.

## Forbidden Anti-Patterns
- No translucent blur, decorative gradients, or low-contrast muted text on critical schedule/status readouts.
