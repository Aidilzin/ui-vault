# Archival Paper / Research Index

Quiet, museum-grade research and curation aesthetic built on crisp white paper, soft neutral block frames, understated gray metadata, and a single deep archival navy signal.

## Core Tokens & Surfaces
- **Canvas & Surfaces:** Crisp paper ground (`#ffffff`), subtle archival card fill (`#f7f7f7`), and clean neutral `1px` borders (`#dedede`).
- **Ink:** Pure black titles (`#000000`), charcoal body (`#333333`), and calm archival gray metadata (`#696969`, `#999999`).
- **Accent:** Deep archival cobalt (`#00075f`) reserved strictly for active connections, links, and collection markers.

## Typography & Layout
- Clean utilitarian grotesque with generous negative space, square-proportioned content blocks, and crisp `3px` radii.

## Motion Rules
- Ultra-brisk `120ms–150ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` transitions on hover and focus; zero ambient idle animations.

## Forbidden Anti-Patterns
- No neon glows, heavy drop shadows, or marketing hero bloat — let collected artifacts carry visual weight.
