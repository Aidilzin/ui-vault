# Orbital Ceramic / Quiet Halo Surfaces

Sculpted dark-ceramic spatial aesthetic inspired by `@01orbital` (clean-room specification), pairing matte stone-charcoal planes with quiet amber/ice halo orbs and whisper-thin luminous seams.

## Core Tokens & Surfaces
- **Canvas & Surfaces:** Deep matte basalt (`#0c0d10`), sculpted ceramic panels (`#14161b`), and subtle `1px` luminous seams (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`).
- **Ink:** Warm porcelain white (`#f1efe9`) and muted ash gray (`#8e8c87`).
- **Accents:** Warm solar corona (`#e8a864`) paired with cool atmospheric blue (`#7aa7d9`) for quiet focal halos.

## Typography & Layout
- Calm geometric sans with generous vertical padding and soft `12px` sculpted card corners.

## Forbidden Anti-Patterns
- No harsh saturated cyber-neon glows or abrupt hard shadows; light must feel diffused and physical.
