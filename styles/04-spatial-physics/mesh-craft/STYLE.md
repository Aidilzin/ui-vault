# Mesh Craft / Luminous Editorial Surface

High-craft modern financial and infrastructure aesthetic pairing crisp porcelain surfaces and deep navy ink with luminous, restrained indigo-violet ambient mesh accents.

## Core Tokens & Surfaces
- **Canvas & Surfaces:** Cool porcelain ground (`#f6f9fc`), pure white elevated cards (`#ffffff`), and crisp cool-gray borders (`#e3e8ee`).
- **Ink:** Deep maritime slate (`#0d253d`), readable charcoal body (`#273951`), and cool steel metadata (`#64748d`).
- **Accents:** Electric blurple primary (`#533afd`, hover `#4434d4`) paired with soft periwinkle (`#b9b9f9`) and warm sand highlights (`#f5e9d4`).

## Typography & Layout
- Crisp geometric sans with tight headline tracking (`-0.03em`) and generous vertical whitespace, framed by subtle diagonal section breaks and `8px` card radii.

## Forbidden Anti-Patterns
- Never let mesh gradients bleed behind dense data tables or form inputs — confine ambient color strictly to hero backdrops and graphic callouts.
- Avoid muddy black drop shadows; use ultra-soft tinted slate elevation (`rgba(13, 37, 61, 0.08)`).
