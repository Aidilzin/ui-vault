# Velvet Chrome Prism

Deep ultraviolet-charcoal ground with iridescent violet-cyan prism border seams and dark optical specimen cards.

## Core Tokens & Surfaces
- **Canvas:** Ultraviolet obsidian (`#07050f`) with deep velvet card planes (`#100c1e`) and specular chrome rims (`rgba(167, 139, 250, 0.24)`).
- **Chromatic Accent:** Spectral violet (`#8b5cf6`) paired with cyan prism highlights (`#22d3ee`) used strictly on borders, active badges, and optical focus rings.
- **Depth:** Subtle glass blur (`12px`) with razor-thin inner top highlights.

## Motion Rules
- Favor pointer-reactive specular sweeps, foil tilt reflections, and smooth radial focus transitions.
- Keep text containers opaque enough (`>= 7:1` contrast) so spectral borders never compromise legibility.

## Forbidden Anti-Patterns
- Never flood entire card backgrounds with bright saturated purple gradients—keep chroma confined to edges, beams, and optical accents.
- Never pair with vintage serif paper aesthetics or 90s beveled chrome.
