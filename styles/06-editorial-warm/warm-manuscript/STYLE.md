# Warm Manuscript / Tactile Parchment

Calm, human-centered reading and writing interface built on warm linen parchment substrates, soft charcoal ink, literary typography, and a single earthy terracotta signal.

## Core Tokens & Surfaces
- **Canvas & Surfaces:** Warm alabaster parchment (`#faf9f5`), soft linen panels (`#f5f0e8`, `#efe9de`), and delicate warm stone hairlines (`#e6dfd8`).
- **Ink:** Warm carbon charcoal (`#141413`), deep espresso secondary (`#3d3d3a`), and muted warm stone (`#6c6a64`, `#8e8b82`).
- **Accents:** Earthy terracotta (`#cc785c`, hover `#a9583e`) used sparingly for primary actions and active indicators.

## Typography & Layout
- Generous editorial measure (`62ch–72ch`), relaxed `1.6` body line-height, and calm `8px–12px` soft corners that invite long-form focus.

## Forbidden Anti-Patterns
- No cold pure `#000000` or harsh `#ffffff` clinical glare, neon cyan glows, or aggressive kinetic skew.
- Never crowd the viewport with dense telemetry chrome — protect breathing room and reading rhythm.
