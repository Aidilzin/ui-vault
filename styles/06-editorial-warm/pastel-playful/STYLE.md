# Pastel Playful / Collaborative Canvas

Expressive multiplayer-tool aesthetic combining warm off-white studio paper, crisp black structural ink, and cheerful pastel block accents in sage, lavender, cream, and peach.

## Core Tokens & Surfaces
- **Canvas & Surfaces:** Warm off-white paper (`#f7f7f5`), crisp white toolbars (`#ffffff`), and clean neutral/ink dividers (`#e6e6e6` / `#000000`).
- **Ink:** Crisp pure black (`#000000`) and deep indigo-slate (`#1f1d3d`).
- **Accents:** Harmonious studio pastel palette — soft sage (`#dceeb1`), lavender (`#c5b0f4`), warm cream (`#f4ecd6`), and peach (`#f3c9b6`) — anchored by dark ink labels for high legibility.

## Typography & Layout
- Friendly geometric sans headlines paired with playful `10px–14px` rounded sticker cards, floating toolbars, and crisp multiplayer cursor tags.

## Forbidden Anti-Patterns
- Never place white text on pastel fills — always pair pastel blocks with dark `#000000` or `#1f1d3d` ink to preserve WCAG contrast.
- Avoid gloomy dark-mode HUD chrome or harsh metallic gradients.
