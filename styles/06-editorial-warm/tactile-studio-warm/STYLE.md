# Tactile Studio Warm / Curated Canvas

Soft, gallery-inspired moodboard aesthetic balancing warm alabaster paper grounds, stone-tinted neutral surfaces, and deep near-black ink with unhurried micro-transitions.

## Core Tokens & Surfaces
- **Canvas & Surfaces:** Warm alabaster (`#fcfbfa`), soft clay-gray tiles (`#f7f5f3`, `#f2f1f0`), and subtle stone borders (`#e8e6e4`).
- **Ink:** Deep ink (`#0d0d0d`), espresso secondary (`#5c5754`), and warm stone metadata (`#6e6a69`, `#9a9796`).
- **Accent:** Matte charcoal pill controls (`#232323` with `#ffffff` text) keeping visual focus entirely on imagery and content.

## Typography & Layout
- Editorial neo-grotesk headings with soft `8px–12px` card radii and masonry/columnar visual grids.

## Motion Rules
- Smooth `150ms–200ms cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` surface interactions paired with `400ms ease-out` image reveals.

## Forbidden Anti-Patterns
- No harsh primary neon borders, heavy chrome bevels, or noisy telemetry HUD chrome.
