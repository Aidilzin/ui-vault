# Wordmark Press / Pure Typography

Radical zero-box typographic interface inspired by `@0nlytype` (clean-room specification) where every control, rule, and ornament is set purely in words, hairlines, and generous whitespace.

## Core Tokens & Surfaces
- **Canvas:** Warm bone paper (`#f8f6f1`), zero card fills (`transparent`), and delicate `1px` typographic rules (`rgba(20, 19, 17, 0.18)`).
- **Ink:** Deep book ink (`#141311`) and editorial footnote gray (`#6b665c`).
- **Accent:** Single vermilion editorial mark (`#c83e2b`) for active states and inline indices.

## Typography & Layout
- Two disciplined typefaces only: high-contrast editorial serif for display words + crisp monospace for indices, fractions, and bracketed `[ACTIONS]`.
- Zero `border-radius` (`0px`) and zero container boxes — alignment and whitespace carry 100% of hierarchy.

## Forbidden Anti-Patterns
- No rounded pill buttons, drop shadows, glass blur, or filled card containers.
- Never introduce icons where a crisp word or typographic symbol (`→`, `×`, `01/04`) works.
