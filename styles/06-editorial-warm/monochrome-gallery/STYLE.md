# Monochrome Gallery / Workspace Paper

Effortlessly calm document and knowledge-base aesthetic combining pure white page substrates, ultra-quiet neutral callout blocks, deep ink legibility, and restrained workspace cobalt accents.

## Core Tokens & Surfaces
- **Canvas & Surfaces:** Pure white document ground (`#ffffff`), quiet warm-stone callout blocks (`#f7f7f5`), and subtle `1px` neutral borders (`#e6e6e4`).
- **Ink:** Deep midnight-charcoal (`#0a1530`), readable body slate (`#37352f`), and muted metadata (`#787774`).
- **Accents:** Calm workspace cobalt (`#0075de`) for active links and focus rings, paired with muted terracotta (`#dd5b00`) for callout tags.

## Typography & Layout
- Clean humanist sans with comfortable `1.6` line-height, `6px` block radii, and keyboard-first slash-command ergonomics.

## Forbidden Anti-Patterns
- No heavy drop shadows, neon borders, or theatrical scroll-jacking that interrupts reading or writing flow.
