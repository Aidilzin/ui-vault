# Gallery Curated Sand

Sunlit limestone and travertine exhibition ground with cool graphite ink, architectural index rules, and calm gallery-grade spacing.

## Core Tokens & Surfaces
- **Gallery Ground:** Warm mineral sand (`#edeae3`) paired with pale travertine plaques (`#f6f4ef`) and stone dividers (`#d6d1c6`).
- **Ink & Accents:** Cool architectural graphite (`#1f2421`), muted slate-olive captions (`#5f635e`), and deep forest-bronze accent (`#3d5a4c`).
- **Hierarchy:** High-contrast editorial display headings over restrained uppercase index labels and wide margins.

## Motion Rules
- Use unhurried, museum-calm easing (`cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.1, 0.25, 1)`) for image curtains, hover previews, and index row reveals.
- Keep motion subtle—never shake, bounce, or flash exhibition typography.

## Forbidden Anti-Patterns
- Never pair with neon terminal green, dark HUD chrome, or harsh saturated primary buttons.
- Avoid cramped dashboard card grids that destroy the exhibition breathing room.
